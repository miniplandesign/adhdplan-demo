How each section works
Home

Pick any month and see its whole picture at a glance: income vs. spending, where the money is going, and what's left to spend, all as charts.

Today

A quick-glance page for right now: how much you've saved, what you still owe, what's safe to spend this month, what's coming up, and which goals are in motion.

Calendar

Tap any day to add a bill, payday, or note. Give it a fixed amount and send it straight into a specific envelope, income, debt or savings — even repeat every month. Bills can be checked off once paid.

Goals

The bigger picture behind all this planning. Tap a goal's status pill to cycle it between not started, in progress, and done.

Savings

Set up as many funds as you like — an emergency cushion, a trip, anything — each with its own progress bar and optional target date.

Debt

List out what you owe. Each debt gets an automatic payoff estimate, and you can sort by highest interest first or smallest balance first.

Envelopes

Twelve reusable tabs — one per month. Log income and money in right away, and give every kind of spending — rent, groceries, coffee, travel, anything — its own envelope with a monthly budget. Add as many categories as you need, and use the "Track spending" button in the sidebar to log something from anywhere.

Yearly review

Tucked under Envelopes. Every month added together into one picture: income vs. spending, savings progress, and debt paid down.

Settings

Your name, your colorway, night mode, backups, and the option to erase everything and start fresh.

Calm Budgeting Philosophy

• Safe to Spend: Avoid math fatigue. Look at Today's Safe-to-Spend number to know exactly what you can spend today without guilt.
• Zero-Drama Envelopes: Set gentle boundaries for categories like Dining and Shopping.
• No-Guilt Reset: If you overspend, don't quit. Adjust next month's allocation or log a quick rebalance.
• 100% Offline & Private: Your data never leaves your device. Export a backup anytime from Settings.
